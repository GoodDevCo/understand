This is not legal advice, and you must not use it as though it were. Nothing here tells you what any particular person should do. Immigration outcomes turn on details you will not know to ask about. What this page is for is helping you understand what people are talking about, avoid making things worse, and get someone to a person who can advise them.

Every fact here comes from a government source, a court record, or the United Nations, and links back to it. Where a figure comes from an advocacy organization instead, it says so.
